# EPIC: Codebase Exploration

This guide is for users who want to understand an existing codebase without necessarily making significant changes. The AI will act as a guide, helping to navigate the project, explain its components, and answer questions.

## Phase 1: Initial Codebase Overview

-   AI will provide a high-level overview of the codebase structure, main technologies, and inferred purpose.
-   AI will highlight key entry points and communication mechanisms.

## Phase 2: Guided Exploration

-   Human will ask specific questions about parts of the codebase (e.g., "How does X work?", "Where is Y implemented?").
-   AI will use its analysis capabilities to explain relevant code sections, architectural patterns, and data flows.

## Phase 3: Deep Dive & Clarification

-   AI will assist in tracing execution paths, explaining complex algorithms, or clarifying design choices.
-   AI will answer follow-up questions and provide additional context as needed.
